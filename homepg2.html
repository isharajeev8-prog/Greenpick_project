About Us

Smarter Farming, Better Harvests

GreenPick aims to simplify and modernize farming practices using advanced technology. By analyzing soil composition and environmental conditions, this system helps you find the most suitable crops for your farmland, so every farming decision is backed by data instead of guesswork.

Our vision is to make smart farming easy for everyone, from small family farms to larger holdings, supporting higher yields, wiser use of resources and a more sustainable future for agriculture.
